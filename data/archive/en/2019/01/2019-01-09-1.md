BUG FIXES

**General**

- Fixed a bug that prevented special event music for Ana’s Bastet Challenge from playing in the main menu
- Fixed a bug that prevented heroes from loading on the victory screen
- Fixed a bug that caused Wrecking Ball’s golden weapon to display incorrectly when using certain skins
