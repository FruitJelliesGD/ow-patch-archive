Overwatch Retail Patch Notes – July 25, 2019

A new patch is now live on Windows PC. Read below to learn more about the latest changes.

To share your feedback, please post in the General Discussion forum

For a list of known issues, visit our Bug Report forum.

For troubleshooting assistance, visit our Technical Support forum.

BUG FIXES

**General**

- Fixed a bug that made it possible in rare circumstances to lose ultimate charge.
- Fixed a bug that prevented Competitive Play groups being found in LFG.
- Fixed rare instances of "avoid as teammate" not working.

**Heroes**

- Fixed a bug that caused Kendoka Genji to briefly t-pose during the outro for his Meditate emote.
