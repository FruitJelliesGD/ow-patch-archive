# Overwatch Retail Patch Notes – April 29, 2020

> 日期: 2020-04-29 · 站点: en · 来源: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2020/04

## None

To share your feedback, please post in the General Discussion forum. For a list of known issues, visit our Bug Report forum. For troubleshooting assistance, visit our Technical Support forum.

## Hero Updates

### Ana（支援）

#### Sleep Dart
- Sleep duration lowered from 6 to 5 seconds

### Mei（输出）

#### Endothermic Blaster
- Freeze stun duration lowered from 1.5 to 1.3 seconds

#### Blizzard
- Duration lowered from 5 to 4.25 seconds

### McCree

#### Flashbang
- Stun duration lowered from 0.85 to 0.7 seconds

### Reinhardt（重装）

#### Earthshatter
- Knockdown duration lowered from 3 to 2.5 seconds

### Roadhog（重装）

#### Whole Hog
- Knockback increased 20%

### Sigma（重装）

#### Accretion
- Knockdown duration is now a fixed 0.8 second instead of scaling with distance
- Cast time reduced from 0.75 to 0.65 seconds
