## Hero Updates

### Ana（支援）

#### Biotic Rifle
- [Console-Only Update] Allied projectile size increased from 0.3 to 0.4

### Brigitte（支援）

*开发者注：On top of the high utility Brigitte brings to her team, she also dishes out a ton of healing and armor. We’re reducing some of this healing and armor to help keep her in line with the other healers.*

#### Repair Pack
- Armor over-heal reduced from 75 armor to 50 armor
- HPS decreased from 60 to 55

### Symmetra（输出）

*开发者注：Increasing Photon Projectors max damage allows Symmetra to play more effectively at a safer range when necessary.*

#### Photon Projector
- Damage increased from 120 to 140

#### Sentry Turret
- [Console-Only Update] Turret DPS reduced from 34 to 30

### Widowmaker（输出）

*开发者注：Widowmaker has become stronger over time following changes to other heroes, such as power reductions to barriers. This change is intended to slow down her shots, reducing her overall DPS.*

#### Widow's Kiss
- Full charge time for scoped shots increased from 0.75 sec to 0.9 sec

### Wrecking Ball（重装）

*开发者注：This change allows Wrecking Ball’s enemies to regain control of their movement more quickly to try and avoid attacks, making Piledriver combos a bit less deadly.*

#### Piledriver
- Affected players’ loss of air control reduced from 1 second to 0.5 seconds
