# Overwatch Retail Patch Notes – September 10, 2020

> 日期: 2020-09-10 · 站点: en · 来源: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2020/09

## Hero Updates

**General**

- Shotgun patterns no longer apply random rotation. This will affect the following heroes:

  - Ashe
  - Doomfist
  - D.Va
  - Reaper
  - Roadhog
  - Torbjörn

*开发者注：We’re making weapons that utilize shotgun patterns more reliable by removing the random rotation applied to each shot. Individual pellets within the shot will still have a tiny amount of variance to their firing angle for the sake of the shotgun weapon feel, but this change will provide significantly more control over aiming these weapons.*

### Roadhog（重装）

*开发者注：When combined with the changes to shotgun patterns, we’re looking to keep Roadhog’s Chain Hook combo reliable, but not overly powerful against heroes with higher health pools.*

#### Scrap Gun
- Projectile damage reduced from 7 to 6 (150 total per shot)

### Torbjörn（输出）

*开发者注：This change to Torbjörn’s secondary fire is aimed at giving it a stronger role at close range. We’re hoping this change allows each shot to feel more impactful, since it has a larger burst. To keep this change in line, we’ve also reduced the damage-per-second.*

#### Rivet Gun
- Recovery increased from 0.6 to 0.8 seconds
- Damage increased from 10.5 to 12.5 per projectile (125 total per shot)
