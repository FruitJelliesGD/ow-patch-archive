# Overwatch Retail Patch Notes – October 13, 2026

> 日期: 2026-10-13 · 站点: en · 来源: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2026/10

## Hotfix Update

This is a hotfix update. Replay codes from the October 6 patch are still available.

## Hero Updates

### Doctrine

#### Eternal Scepter
- Damage and healing reduced from 60 to 55.

#### Invigorating Drones
- Duration reduced from 6 to 5 seconds.

### Sombra（输出）

#### Machine Pistol
- Damage reduced from 7 to 6.5.

#### Cyberspace
- Explosion damage reduced from 40 to 20.
- Cooldown increased from 12 to 13 seconds.

### Roadhog（重装）

#### Scrap Gun
- Center projectile damage increased from 34 to 40.

#### Take a Breather
- Cooldown reduced from 2 to 1.25 seconds.
- Damage reduction increased from 35% to 40%.
- Max duration increased from 2 to 2.5 seconds.

### Kiriko（支援）

- Base health increased from 225 to 250.

### D.Mon（重装）

- Base armor reduced from 275 to 250.
