## Reign of Talon - Season 5: A Grim Doctrine

### New Event - Mystery Mayhem: Graveyard Games

A freakishly fun event rises from the grave -- Mystery Madness: Graveyard Games!

For four wicked weeks, complete challenges to earn ravishing rewards, and cast Offerings to help determine which shadowy characters are first to be summoned into a brand new limited-time mode.

**Ghoulishly Good Challenges**

Complete challenges to collect rewards. Every challenge conquered unearths spooky swag, as well as Offerings you can use in voting.

**Offer Up Your Devotion**

Offerings decide which characters crawl into the Graveyard Games mode next. Earn as many Offerings as you can, and direct them to any of the available heroes you want; you can spread them out, or pledge your allegiance to just one!

### New Arcade Gamemode - Mystery Madness: Graveyard Games

Mystery Madness is our new Halloween Event mode, combining elements of Mystery Heroes, Junkenstein’s Lab, and Stadium. But there's an added twist: you gain Powers when you die. Mystery Madness is 5v5 Open Queue but with guaranteed 1-2-2 Role Composition and has the game format of Best-of-3 Control.

At the start of a match, you are assigned a random role and given 3 hero choices within that role. If you like to leave your chances up to fate, there is also a random button that will pick between 3 heroes and grant you a small boost.

Once the match starts, you will gain experience passively and from eliminations and assists. Once you gain enough experience, you will earn a level. When you die or on round transition, you will earn Power Choices, picking between 2 Powers, equal to the number of levels you accumulated since you last died. Powers are similar to Perks or Stadium Items / Powers, and can be either Common, Epic, or Legendary. Commons are comparable to Minor Perks or Rare to Epic Stadium items, and are usually supportive effects. Epics are comparable to Major Perks or Stadium Powers, and are usually the cornerstone of your build. Legendaries go even further beyond, and can lead to some truly crazy combinations. Some standout spooky powers are Jump Scare, which turns you into a Prop while out of combat, letting you flank and frighten your enemies, or Zomnic Summoner, which lets you throw a grenade that summons a Zomnic upon detonation. On death, you will also gain 10% Ultimate Charge and respawn with Overhealth equal to 40% of your Max Life and 30% Move Speed while out of combat.

Our newest spooky support, Doctrine, will also be playable in Mystery Madness: Graveyard Games!

## General Updates

### Custom Restricted Words

- Added new Option (Custom Restricted Words) to Social -> Text Chat.
- Added information regarding Custom Restricted Words to Text Profanity Filters info dialog and Report dialog.
- Player-sent chat messages containing a restricted word(s) will be hidden with a "Possible Bad Message (Custom)" warning, with the option to reveal the message.

*开发者注：Developer Comments: Our automated systems do their best to hide disruptive chat by default, but this feature gives all players the ability to specify exact words and phrases that they don't want to see in text chat, which applies to all text chat channels (Whisper/Group/Team/Match).*

### Unvaulted Battle Passes

You can now progress Unvaulted Battle Passes from past seasons alongside the current season's Battle Pass!

-
From the Home Screen, navigate to Battle Passes > Legacy Pass to see the full list of available Passes.

-
Previously purchased Battle Passes can be activated and progressed. Any progress that was made when the Battle Pass was initially run is carried over when the Pass becomes unvaulted.

  - 2026: Season 4 Battle Pass will not be available to activate at launch and will be made available in the midseason.
  - Certain rewards such as Mythic Skins, Mythic Prisms, Overwatch Coins, XP Boosts, and Player Titles are no longer obtainable in unvaulted passes.
-
Legacy Seasons 1-15 Battle Passes are available to purchase in the shop under the new Unvaulted Battle Pass Tab.

  - Locked Unvaulted Passes will arrive in later seasons.
-
In order to earn Battle Pass XP towards an Unvaulted Pass, you must own it and then activate it from the Legacy Pass Menu.

  - Only 1 Unvaulted Pass can be active at a time to progress alongside the current season's Battle Pass.

## Competitive Play Updates

### Top 500 Challenger System Updates

- Top 500 Challenger Leaderboard sorting default changed to Rank (from Challenger Score).
- End of Season title rewards are now referencing Rank instead of Challenger Score.
- Removed Heat Bonus.

*开发者注：Developer Comments: This greater focus on Rank over Challenger Score is intended to reward our most skilled players with less of a focus on play time. Challenger Score remains only as a requirement to be on the leaderboard. Only the 500 players with the highest Challenger Score will be on the Leaderboard, but it's now possible to be Rank 1 with the lowest Challenger Score of those 500 players if you have the highest actual Rank. We believe this new direction will still prevent high ranked accounts with very low playtime from being on the Leaderboard, while now more accurately rewarding the best players that play throughout a season.*

### Roadhog（重装）

*开发者注：Developer Comments: We had two main goals with this Roadhog rework: to reduce the frustration that comes out of how lethal Chain Hook ends up being on most heroes, and to give Roadhog a new tool to help him aid his team as a more traditional Tank would. Changing his primary fire to shoot in a 2-burst cadence alongside slight adjustments to Chain Hook should preserve the ability’s strength as a displacement tool while dampening its lethality. Meanwhile, Roadhog’s new ability, Trash Compactor, brings new utility for him and his team that feeds back into Roadhog being a lethal force on the battlefield. While Trash Compactor is active, Roadhog vacuums up projectiles in front of him, converting them into an explosive projectile to fling back at enemies. Operating as a strong defensive and offensive ability, Trash Compactor should help point Roadhog towards making game-swinging plays with more vectors than just trying to one-shot-combo heroes.*

### Sombra（输出）

*开发者注：Developer Comments: Sombra is being brought into the Support role with this latest rework. In the past, her identity as a disruptive mastermind was at odds with the value a Damage hero was expected to bring. Moving Sombra to the Support role should allow us to fully lean into her hero fantasy while keeping important pillars of her gameplay intact. One notable change of the rework is the removal of Hack as an ability. Facing Sombra often meant being unexpectedly locked out of your abilities from time to time. While this can create fun moments of cancelling key abilities for the Sombra player, most players found it jarring, frustrating, and difficult to deal with. By removing Hack from her main abilities, we brought in a new debuff Sombra can inflict on enemies: Weaken, which halves an enemy’s own damage and healing output. A well-timed weaken can turn the tide of battle, much like Hack was able to, without completely locking an enemy out of their own abilities.*
