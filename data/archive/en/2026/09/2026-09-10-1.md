# Overwatch Retail Patch Notes – September 10, 2026

> 日期: 2026-09-10 · 站点: en · 来源: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2026/09

## Hotfix Patch

This is a hotfix patch. Replay codes from the September 8 patch are still available.

## Bug Fixes

- Resolved an issue with Ramattra's Ravenous Vortex that caused additional unintended delay before it could be detonated.
- Fixed a bug with Jetpack Cat that allowed Purr to go through enemy barriers.
- Fixed an issue that affected the range of Vendetta's Palatine Fang in some instances.
