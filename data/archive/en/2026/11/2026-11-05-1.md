# Overwatch Retail Patch Notes – November 5, 2026

> 日期: 2026-11-05 · 站点: en · 来源: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2026/11

## Hotfix Update

This is a hotfix update. Replay codes from the November 3 patch are still available.

## Hero Updates

### Wrecking Ball（重装）

#### Grappling Claw
- Base speed limit for Wrecking Ball while retracting Grappling Claw increased by 15%.
- Allied speed boosts now increase the speed limit for Wrecking Ball while retracting Grappling Claw.

## Bug Fixes

### General

- Players who did not reach Checkpoint 4 in a previous Competitive Drive should no longer have a signature equipped which they cannot unequip.

### Heroes

- In a previous update, fixed an issue where pilot D.Mon would be resurrected without ultimate charge, thereby preventing D.Mon from immediately getting back into her mech.

### Stadium

- Fixed an issue where the Scoreboard and related screens would not display properly.
