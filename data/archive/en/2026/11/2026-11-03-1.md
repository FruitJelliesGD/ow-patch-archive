## General Updates

### Gameplay Sabotage Detection

We're improving our tools to combat gameplay sabotage in Overwatch. We have made improvements to our current detection tools that allow us to more accurately detect players engaging in disruptive behaviors, and we've already taken action against many players detected by these systems. Examples of gameplay sabotage include consistently and intentionally failing to contribute to team fights, repeatedly jumping off the map, and intentionally blocking team progress. We will also continue to iterate on these detection methods so that we can catch even more players who are intentionally making other players' experiences worse.

As part of this update, we have also rolled out 'Thank You' messages for reporting a player for disruptive behaviors. You will see these show up in the game's Notification Hub if you report a player for disruptive behaviors, and appropriate actions have been taken against them.

- Internal cooldown reduced from 1.25 to 1 second.

*开发者注：Developer Comments: Doctrine’s healing output has remained high relative to the additional utility he provides. Increasing Eternal Sceptre’s Primary Fire recovery time reduces his overall output, while the reduced Infused values lower his burst potential. Increasing the cooldowns of Shrouded Momentum and Infuse places greater emphasis on thoughtful use of both abilities throughout engagements while maintaining their individual power.*

#### Eternal Scepter
- Primary Fire recovery time increased from 0.6 to 0.65
- Infused Damage reduced from 90 to 80.
- Infused Healing reduced from 120 to 110.

#### Shrouded Momentum
- Cooldown increased from 8 to 10 seconds.

#### Infuse
- Cooldown increased from 8 to 9 seconds.

### Illari（支援）

*开发者注：Developer Comments: Recent projectile size changes have reduced Illari’s overall offensive effectiveness. Reducing Solar Rifle’s Primary Fire charge time places less emphasis on individual shots while increasing her overall damage output, reinforcing her strength as a high-damage Support. Healing Pylon’s output is also being increased to strengthen Illari’s defensive setups when positioned effectively.*

#### Solar Rifle
- Time to maximum charge reduced from 0.65 to 0.575.

#### Healing Pylon
- Heal per projectile increased from 40 to 50.

### Jetpack Cat（支援）

*开发者注：Developer Comments: Jetpack Cat’s survivability has remained strong alongside her high aerial mobility. Reducing her base health limits the survivability and aggression enabled by that mobility while preserving it as a core strength. Frenetic Flight’s acceleration is also being increased to enable faster repositioning, while its reduced fuel regeneration limits how frequently it can be used.*

- Base health reduced from 225 to 200.

#### Frenetic Flight
- Acceleration increased from 25 to 28 meters per second.
- Fuel regeneration rate reduced from 25% to 20% per second.

### Sombra（支援）

*开发者注：Developer Comments: Hotfix’s duration provided enough uptime that choosing when and who to use it on carried less weight. Reducing its duration places greater emphasis on those decisions while retaining its effectiveness when used well. Cyberspace’s Weaken effect is also being reduced to provide affected enemies with more opportunity to respond. With the recent changes to health pack interactions between Sombra’s Hack and Hanzo’s Sonic Disruption, EMP will now override all enemy effects on health packs to preserve its effectiveness as an Ultimate.*

#### Hotfix
- Duration reduced from 12 to 10 seconds.

#### Cyberspace
- Weaken effect reduced from 50% to 40%.

#### EMP
- Now overrides enemy hack effects on health packs.
