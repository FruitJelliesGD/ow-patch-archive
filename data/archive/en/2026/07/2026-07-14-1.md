## Hero Updates

Hero Updates

## Tank（重装）

### Doomfist（重装）

*开发者注：Empowered Rocket Punch's increased follow-through size was hitting targets far away from the initial target. We're reducing the bonus size while retaining some additional range and radius to preserve its power fantasy.*

#### Rocket Punch
- Empowered Punch collateral range gain reduced 75% to 40%.
- Empowered Punch collateral radius gain reduced from 50% to 40%.

### Junker Queen（重装）

*开发者注：Commanding Shout is Junker Queen's primary source of survivability and team protection. Its cooldown begins after the effect expires, resulting in a longer time between uses. We're reducing the cooldown to increase its availability during engagements.*

#### Commanding Shout
- Cooldown reduced from 12 to 10 seconds. (5v5)
- Cooldown reduced from 15 to 13 seconds. (6v6)

### Mauga（重装）

*开发者注：Mauga could maintain high accuracy when firing a single Chaingun for extended periods. Adding dynamic spread to individual Chainguns encourages switching between weapons to manage spread. This change does not affect accuracy while firing both weapons simultaneously.*

#### Chainguns
- Maximum spread while firing individual Chainguns increased from 1 to 1.5.
- Number of shots until maximum spread increased from 0 to 30 shots.

### Ramattra（重装）

*开发者注：Increasing Ravenous Vortex's explosion damage rewards more precise detonations. We're also reducing Block's cooldown to allow Ramattra to block immediately after a single punch instead of two, improving the flow of Nemesis Form.*

#### Ravenous Vortex
- Explosion damage increased from 15 to 30.

#### Block (Nemesis Form)
- Cooldown reduced from 1 to 0.5 seconds.

### Sigma（重装）

*开发者注：Sigma remains durable when successfully rotating through his defensive tools. Reducing his shield health lowers his survivability when opponents are able to break through his defenses.*

- Shield health reduced from 275 to 250.

- 威能 **Hyper Regeneration** —— 调整
  - Damage conversion reduced from 40% to 30%.
## Damage（输出）

### Cassidy（输出）

*开发者注：Silver Bullet's additional projectile size improved the perk's effectiveness by making the empowered shot significantly easier to land. We're removing that bonus while retaining its potential damage.*

- 威能 **Silver Bullet** —— 调整
  - Projectile size reduced from 0.17 to 0.07.
### Freja（输出）

*开发者注：Reducing Take Aim's explosion delay lowers the time between impact and damage, creating more consistent follow-up opportunities while reducing the bolt's disruptive potential when hit by it. The other changes are aimed at improving the flow between Take Aim and Revdraw Crossbow’s primary fire.*

#### Revdraw Crossbow
- Fire Rate increased from 5 to 5.5.

#### Take Aim
- Recovery reduced from 0.5 to 0.4.
- Explosion delay reduced from 1 to 0.8 seconds.

### Reaper（输出）

*开发者注：Reaper's reduced health pool has limited his ability to sustain through extended engagements. Increasing the effectiveness of his passive restores some of that durability while rewarding aggressive play.*

- 威能 **Soul Reaving** —— 调整
  - Pickup radius of Soul Globes increased from 5 to 8 meters.
#### The Reaping
- Lifesteal increased from 25% to 30%.

### Shion（输出）

*开发者注：Reducing Execution's recovery time better aligns the downtime after using the ability with when Shion can fire again. Joyride's impact damage is also being reduced to lower the effectiveness of continuously ramming targets and the combo potential of immediately detonating the bike after impact.*

#### Execution
- Recovery time reduced from 0.4 to 0.3 seconds.

#### Joyride
- Impact damage while riding reduced from 60 to 30.

### Sierra（输出）

*开发者注：Anchor Drone is being adjusted to support more frequent deployment and repositioning opportunities. Its cooldown and duration are being reduced, while its health is being increased to improve its survivability in combat.*

#### Anchor Drone
- Cooldown reduced from 12 to 11 seconds.
- Duration reduced from 10 to 9 seconds.
- Health increased from 80 to 125.

### Vendetta（输出）

*开发者注：Vendetta has continued to underperform since the significant changes made several seasons ago. Reverting Palatine Fang's overhead swing range restores some of her ability to close the distance and secure eliminations.*

#### Palatine Fang
- Overhead swing bonus range increased from 2.0 to 2.5 meters.

### Venture（输出）

*开发者注：Clobber's cooldown is being reduced to shorten the delay before Venture can use the ability again and better align it with its recovery time. This increases the frequency with which Clobber can be used within engagements and improves its synergy with the Covered in Dirt Major Perk.*

#### Clobber (Quick Melee)
- Cooldown/Recovery reduced from 1.6 to 1.2 seconds.

## Support（支援）

### Ana（支援）

*开发者注：Ana's self-sustain has decreased following the removal of the Support passive's reduced health regeneration delay. Increasing Biotic Grenade's healing strengthens its defensive utility while also increasing its offensive impact.*

#### Biotic Grenade
- Explosion damage and healing increased from 75 to 90.

### Brigitte（支援）

*开发者注：Adding a burst of instant healing when Inspire is triggered allows Brigitte to provide more immediate healing while rewarding players for consistent Inspire triggers.*

#### Inspire
- Now grants 12 instant healing on trigger.
- Healing over time reduced from 60 to 45.

### Kiriko（支援）

*开发者注：The reduced recovery time on Healing Ofuda improved the feel and consistency of Kiriko's healing. However, her overall healing output has become too high, so we're reducing the base healing while preserving the improved responsiveness.*

#### Healing Ofuda
- Healing per projectile reduced from 13 to 12.

### Lúcio（支援）

*开发者注：Lúcio's overall effectiveness has been reduced over time to account for his uniquely powerful speed utility. Increasing his weapon damage strengthens his offensive capabilities without increasing the strength of his utility.*

#### Sonic Amplifier
- Damage per projectile increased from 20 to 22.

## Bug Fixes

### General

- Fixed an issue causing Shion's Hero Challenges to not be sorted alphabetically.
- Fixed an issue with Mauga where his reticle spread could become inaccurate when swapping between his guns.
- Fixed an issue where Sierra’s Trailblazer drone could be interrupted by stun abilities.

### Stadium

- Fixed an issue where Junker Queen's Bloodhound Mask wasn't properly granting bonus Weapon Power from Wounds.
- Fixed an issue where Doomfist's Friendly Fissure caused Meteor Strike to deal full damage regardless of distance.
- Fixed an issue where Cassidy's Gunslinger Grit was triggering before the reload had completed.
- Fixed an issue where critically healing an ally after dealing Ability Damage with Cybervenom would apply Healing Reduction to allies.
- Fixed an issue where Lucio's Hip Hop wasn't properly working with Wall Jump on Release option toggled off.
- Fixed an issue where Mei's Frosting wasn't properly granting Ultimate Charge.
- Fixed an issue where Ramattra's Barrier wasn't scaling with Ability Power.
- Fixed an issue where Mei's Slowball would visually appear as though it was always the empowered projectile if the Power was sold while buff was active.
- Fixed an issue where Kiriko's Leaf on the Wind would target sometimes target enemies.
- In a previous update, fixed an issue where Moira's Spectral Beam had an incorrect effectiveness value.
