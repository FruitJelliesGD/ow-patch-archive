# Overwatch Retail Patch Notes – July 30, 2026

> 日期: 2026-07-30 · 站点: en · 来源: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2026/07

## Limited-Time Mode Specific Updates

The following patch notes are applicable only to a specific, limited-time game mode. The documented changes do not apply to pre-existing queues, unless otherwise noted.

## Hero Updates

### General Changes

In-Combat Healing Reduction reduced from 30% to 15% for all heroes.

### Tank Role Passive

Ultimate charge reduction when damaging or healing tank targets increased from 20% to 40%.

### Subrole Passives

Bruiser Movement speed buff reduced from 20% to 15%. Initiator Healing reduced from 50 to 40. Cooldown reduced from 5 to 4 seconds. Flanker Additional health from Health Packs reduced from 75 to 50. Medic Healing conversion reduced from 40% to 30%.
