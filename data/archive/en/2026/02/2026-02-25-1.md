# Overwatch Retail Patch Notes - February 25, 2026

> 日期: 2026-02-25 · 站点: en · 来源: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2026/02

## Bug Fix Update

This is a bug fix patch and contains no game balance updates. In-game replay codes and highlights from the previous patch are still available.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue with Domina's Photon Magnum beam damage not calculating correctly against armor health.
- Fixed a bug with Pharah's Perk Rocket Salvo that allowed users to fire more rockets than intended.
- Fixed an issue in 6v6 Open Queue that resulted in Stalwart Tanks not having Knockback Resistance.
- Fixed an issue in 6v6 Open Queue that resulted in Bruiser Tanks missing their Critical Hit Resistance.
