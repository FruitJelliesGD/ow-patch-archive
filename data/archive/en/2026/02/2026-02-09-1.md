## General Updates: Comms/Ping Updates

### Praise

- "Praise" added as a new default function of the Comms Wheel.
- Tapping the Comms Wheel button now tells your allies how awesome they are with Praise voice lines that are unique to each hero.
- By default, the Comms button functionality now mimics the functionality of the Ping Button, meaning there is a very short delay (.18 seconds by default) between pressing the button and the wheel opening.
- This new functionality can be disabled in the Options with the setting "Enable Quick Press Praise". The delay can be modified with the setting "Comms Wheel Press Delay".
- This short delay is immediately canceled when any mouse movement or right stick movement is detected, for players that want to flick to a Comms Wheel option.
- Praise can be assigned to the Comms Wheel in the Options, but it is not assigned to the Comms Wheel by default.

### New Contextual Voice Lines and Functionality

- Heroes now emit voice lines that suggest setup locations to their team when players use neutral pings during the Setup Phase while defending in Attack/Defend modes.
- Heroes now have more specific internal monologue voice lines for when they request healing and no supports are available to help.
- Heroes on defense in Escort and Hybrid are now more likely to notice when a payload is being pushed by a sneaky player and call it out with a voice line.
- Heroes will now automatically call out to their team with a voice line when they detect an invisible hero nearby (Sombra).

  - This uses the same cooldown and tuning as when heroes detect Widowmaker aiming at them and warn their allies with "sniper" voice lines.
- Heroes can aim at an allied Jetpack Cat and ping her to request a pick up at their location with a voice line.
- The "On My Way" Comms now prints an ETA in seconds in text chat when aiming at an allied hero based on the speaker's speed.

### Bug Fixes

- The Ping System's spam protection now works in the Waiting For Players phase of a match.
- Supports no longer hear the healing ping sound effect when another support on their team pings an injured ally.
- Thanking a support for the "Not So Thankless" challenge should now more reliably count for challenge completion.

## General Updates: Advanced Info Panel

- Added all 5 new heroes.
- All Cleanses in the Advanced Info Panel have been separated between Lesser Cleanses which are much more common and Greater Cleanses which are more rare and can cleanse Stuns. The definitions of these keywords have been updated.

- Added the following new Keywords to abilities where relevant:

  - Aura - An Energy ability that gains resource whenever dealing damage or healing.

    - Example: Mizuki's Remedy Aura
  - Break - Removes Tether effects.

    - Examples: Jetpack Cat's Catnapper, Lifeweaver's Life Grip, Reinhardt's Charge, Roadhog's Chain Hook
  - Displace - Prevents a hero from taking actions while moving them, interrupts Channeled abilities, and is not Cleansable.

    - Examples: Doomfist's Rocket Punch, Reinhardt's Charge, Roadhog's Chain Hook
  - Heat - Ceases to function when the Heat gauge is full and cools off automatically when not firing.

    - Examples: Emre's Siphon Blaster, Orisa's Augmented Fusion Driver
  - Immobilize - Removes control of hero movement.

    - Examples: Hazard's Downpour, Lucio's Soundwave, Wrecking Ball's Piledriver, Zarya's Graviton Surge
  - Tether - Attaches a hero to a location or hero within a radius until the tether ends.

    - Examples: Jetpack Cat's Lifeline, Jetpack Cat's Catnapper, Mauga's Cage Fight, Mizuki's Binding Chain

## Competitive Play Updates

### Rank Reset

- With 5 new heroes joining the fray, alongside several new perks and balance changes for existing heroes, this soft reset is designed to put players on an even playing field and give them a chance to climb in a chaotic new environment where everyone is learning together!
- All ranks in Role Queue and Open Queue in Competitive Play have been reset to Unranked, and all players will need to complete their respective 10 placement matches for each role.
- Hero Skill Ratings have also been soft reset.
- This Rank Reset did not change the Rank Distribution, so players can expect to see a similar number of players in each Rank once everyone is calibrated again.
- Rewards:
- Crimson Wolf replaces Galactic as this year's Competitive Weapon Variant and will be available to be unlocked with Competitive Points after the first two weeks of the season!
- Galactic Weapon Variants join the Gold and Jade Weapon Variants as Legacy Rewards.
- Achieving Diamond or above in Role Queue or Open Queue will now unlock Doomfist's Crimson Wolf skin.
- All Competitive Points earned in the 2025 Competitive Year have been converted into Legacy Competitive Points.

### Top 500 Challenger Updates

- We heard your feedback about the name change, and we agree "Top 500" was too iconic to abandon, so we've combined the old and new names.

- Sort By Rank:

  - The Top 500 Challenger leaderboard can now be sorted by Rank.

    - Rewards are awarded based on Challenger Score.
    - Only players with enough Challenger Score to be on the leaderboard are sorted by Rank.

### Rule Changes

- Challenger Score is no longer awarded based on the highest ranked player in the match.

  - Challenger Score is now always awarded based on your own rank.
- Diamond removed from Top 500 Challenger eligibility
- The challenge required for Top 500 Challenger eligibility has been swapped from Seasoned Competitor to Adept Competitor.
- Challenger Score deduction on loss increased from 33% to 40%

*开发者注：We quickly realized this design choice was a mistake when we heard the player feedback that the leaderboard should prioritize rank much more highly than it did at Season 20 launch, and this choice meant that the highest ranked players were often donating a considerable amount of Challenger Score in their games to lower ranked players. This change means that we can tune the highest ranks to gain considerably more Challenger Score and ensures that only the highest ranked players benefit from these rewards. These increased payouts for the highest ranks now account for both the difficulty of maintaining these ranks and the increased queue times associated with these ranks. Since we redistributed the Ranks in Season 18 to allow for more players at our highest Ranks (Master, Grandmaster, Champion) we agree with the community feedback that Diamond is not prestigious enough for the leaderboard. This change means that 250 total wins in Competitive Play are required to appear in Top 500 Challenger instead of 750. With Challenger Score as a requirement, the 750 win requirement was unnecessarily high.*

### Rewards

- Player Icons return as a reward for Top 500 Challenger.

  - New icons will be available at each Rank Reset, meaning that a new icon becomes available to earn every 3 seasons.
  - Placing in Top 500 Challenger during the Rise of Talon Season 1, 2, or 3 will grant the Conquest Top 500 Challenger player icon.

  - We're continuing to investigate solutions to this problem and we apologize to those players that are unable to use this feature at this time.

### Bug Fixes

- Fixed the Friends Leaderboard being organized randomly.

*开发者注：We hope these updates show our commitment to updating this system based on player feedback, but this is also a good time to address some of the feedback we've heard that probably isn't a viable path for us today. We've heard some of our highest rank players request that we decay ranks at the start of each season like we did before the launch of Overwatch 2. Before Season 9 of Overwatch 2, Rank and MMR were not directly linked and could diverge considerably. After Season 9, we committed to Rank and MMR being linked in the Core game because we believe showing you the most accurate representation of your skill is important to the competitive integrity of the system. That's still the case today, and it's why the type of pseudo-Rank decay that was applied to high rank players at that time (artificially displaying them at a lower SR than their MMR) is not an option for us today. If we used that type of decay today, we'd be lowering the MMR of all of the highest ranked players every season. This has a lot of knock on effects, but it would be effectively creating more severe versions of the Rank Resets we do every 3 seasons today, but only for players above a specified MMR. We see this as undesirable because of the negative impact it would have on match quality every season for our highest ranked players. We appreciate all of your feedback about this feature so far and we'll continue to listen for ways we can improve it going forward.*
