# Overwatch Retail Patch Notes – June 25, 2026

> 日期: 2026-06-25 · 站点: en · 来源: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2026/06

## Hotfix Update

This is a hotfix containing gameplay updates and bug fixes. Replay codes from the June 16 patch are still available.

## Hero Updates

### Shion（输出）

#### Execution
- Projectile size reduced from 0.17 to 0.07.
- Recovery time increased from 0 to 0.4s.

## Stadium Updates

Following the recent sweeping Stadium changes, we've been keeping a close eye on the performance of all heroes, their powers, and items. We're tuning down some early overperformers while buffing some of the underperforming new powers to help them find a clearer niche. We'll continue to monitor some of the more frustrating powers and items and make adjustments as necessary.

### General

Three Tap Tommygun Cost increased to 10500 (Up from 10000). Salvaged Slugs Ammo Restore Chance increased to 60% (Up from 50%). Healing increased to 20% (Up from 15%).

## Bug Fixes

### General

In a previous update, fixed a bug that resulted in Ashe's Take Aim damage falling off too quickly.

### Stadium

Fixed a bug where Reaper's Wall of Life and Death Power would not heal allies if they were not within range upon initial cast. Fixed a bug where casting Dragonblade while Deflecting with Dragon's Breath and Iaido Strike Powers equipped would not properly trigger Dragon's Breath. Fixed a bug with Ashe's Calamity Power that allowed players to acquire more ammo than intended. Fixed a bug with Moira's Chain Grasp Power where Biotic Grasp would only chain to 1 additional target. Fixed a bug where D.Va's Dae-Hyun Detonator Hero Item would not grant 15% starting Ultimate Charge. Fixed a bug where Mei's Ice Sheet Hero Item was not granting the correct stats. Fixed a bug where Ana's Cluster Core Item was not granting Ability Power. Fixed a bug where Soldier: 76's Rapid Response Radius Item did not increase Biotic Field's duration. Fixed a bug where Kiriko's Good Fortune Power did not work with Projectile Speed. Fixed a bug where Biotic Bullseye's effect would trigger off Three Tap Tommygun's DOT. Fixed a bug where Exo Springs still counted as a Gadget for certain items. Fixed a bug where Wuyang's Waveshatter didn't affect Splash Strike's damage. Fixed a bug where Genji's Dragon Strike would prevent Dragon Blade's Ultimate VO to play. Fixed a bug where Genji's Cybernetic Speed Power granted less Attack Speed than intended. Fixed a bug where Reinhardt's Iron Drift did not properly knock back enemies when cancelling Charge. Fixed a bug where Wuyang's Current Confluence would heal allies for less than intended. Fixed a bug where using Gadgets on D.Va would not trigger certain Item effects. Fixed a bug where combining Necrotic Repellant and Repulsion Greaves would cause Repulsion Greave's knockback effect to also hinder. Fixed a bug where Moira's Spectral Beam power would shorten Coalescence Duration if used in quick succession.
