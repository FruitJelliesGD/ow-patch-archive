## Bug Fix Update

This is a bug fix update. Replay codes have been wiped.

## 4v4 Evolved

4v4 Evolved is a limited time team deathmatch where it's all Emres with even starts, tweaked to make everyone a little more lethal when you lock in. That's right - it's Emre vs Emre, slaying each other in classic FPS style: spawn, engage, adapt, repeat. Put your skills to the test and finish the fight from April 28 - May 11!
