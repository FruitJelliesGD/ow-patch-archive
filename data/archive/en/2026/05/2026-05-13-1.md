# Overwatch Retail Patch Notes – May 14, 2026

> 日期: 2026-05-13 · 站点: en · 来源: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2026/05

## Bug Fix Hotfix Patch

This is a bug fixing hotfix patch. All replay codes from the May 12th patch are still available.

## Stadium Updates

### Jetpack Cat（支援）

- Increased currency gain from healing and damage by 7.14%.

## Bug Fixes

### General

- Fixed an issue where recipients of Nano Boost from Ana's Mythic 'Ra' Skin would not see the appropriate visual effects.

### Stadium

- Fixed an issue where Freja's Mini Bola from Lille Fælde was doing more damage than intended. Lille Fælde has been re-enabled.
- Fixed an issue where Juno's Blink Boosts could constantly trigger On Ability effects during the last charge. Blink Boosts has been re-enabled.
- Fixed an issue where Backstabber was granting additional bleed procs on Critical Hit. Backstabber has been re-enabled.
- Fixed an issue where toggling Ramattra's Void Barrier would trigger On Ability effects.
