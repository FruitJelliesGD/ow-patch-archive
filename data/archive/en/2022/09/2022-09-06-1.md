# Overwatch Retail Patch Notes - September 6, 2022

> 日期: 2022-09-06 · 站点: en · 来源: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2022/09

## OVERWATCH CREDITS LAST CHANCE EVENT

The final moment to spend credits on Overwatch seasonal cosmetics is here!

Starting September 13 at 11:00 A.M. PDT, we’ll be opening the vault of seasonal unlocks for a last chance to spend credits. Remaining credits will be transferred to Overwatch 2 to use on items in the Hero Gallery.

Make sure to grab all your Overwatch seasonal unlock before the event ends October 2!

## OVERWATCH LEAGUE SUMMER SHOWDOWN

Celebrate the Overwatch League Summer Showdown with the Genji Happi legendary skin! This skin is purchasable for a limited time with Overwatch League Tokens.

## BUGS

- Resolved a bug with Baptiste's Arctic Ops portrait
- Fixed an issue where some skins did not display the correct name when localized
