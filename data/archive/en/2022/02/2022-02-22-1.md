# Overwatch Retail Patch Notes – February 22, 2022

> 日期: 2022-02-22 · 站点: en · 来源: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2022/02

## REAPER'S CODE OF VIOLENCE CHALLENGE

Discover Reaper’s origins with the new Overwatch short story, “Code of Violence” by Brandon Easton. During Reaper’s Code of Violence Challenge, earn limited-time rewards, including the new Dusk Reaper skin, by playing games and watching Overwatch streams on Twitch through March 8! Learn more about the challenge on the Overwatch website.

## NEW SOUNDTRACK: OVERWATCH: HEROES & VILLAINS

[The Overwatch: Heroes & Villains soundtrack](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLAYFVhxsaqDvq0C5ux3DkbPrFpsfN_AKC) is now available, featuring music from the origin stories, archives events, and trailers of Overwatch! Find the albums on your favorite music streaming services. For more details see the Overwatch News website or check out the QR code on the main menu. Streaming platform availability may vary by region.

## BUG FIXES

### General

- Fixed an issue where the voice chat icon would not show when speaking in a mid/low range
- Fixed an issue where the left-most voting card could not be selected

### Maps

Eichenwalde

- Fixed an issue that caused the piano to be too loud when hit

### Heroes

Ashe

- Fixed an issue where a high-pitched beeping sound would play during an enemy Ashe's ultimate

Echo

- Fixed a bug that allowed Echo to clone an enemy Echo in specific circumstances

Hanzo

- Fixed a bug caused by using Storm Arrow and Sonic together that wouldn't show an enemy's location if it connected before for the ricochet

Soldier 76

- Auspicious Skin - Fixed a bug that caused Soldier 76's teeth to appear missing
