# Overwatch 2 Retail Patch Notes - November 26, 2024

> 日期: 2024-11-25 · 站点: en · 来源: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2024/11

## Bug Fixes

### General

Fixed an issue causing the Hero Mastery courses for Brigitte and Cassidy to be unavailable. These can now be played again. Fixed an issue with the Stockholm Sunset Name Card. In a previous update, fixed an issue where some players were missing first-person primary fire sounds on some heroes.
