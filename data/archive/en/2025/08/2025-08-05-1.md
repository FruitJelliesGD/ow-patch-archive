# Overwatch 2 Retail Patch Notes - August 5, 2025

> 日期: 2025-08-05 · 站点: en · 来源: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2025/08

## Bug Fix Patch

This is a bug fix patch and contains no hero updates. In-Game Replay Codes and Highlights from the previous patch are removed.

## Bug Fixes

### General

- Fixed an issue where text chat was displaying unintended colors.

### Stadium

Orisa

- Fixed an issue where Orisa's Energy Javelin cooldown was not properly reduced when using cooldown-related mods.

Kiriko

- Fixed an issue where Kiriko's "Clone Conjuration" power failed to grant her clone increased health based on ability powers.
