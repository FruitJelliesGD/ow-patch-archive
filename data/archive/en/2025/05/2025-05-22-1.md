# Overwatch 2 Retail Patch Notes – May 22, 2025

> 日期: 2025-05-22 · 站点: en · 来源: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2025/05

## Hotfix Balance Update

This is a game balance hotfix. Replay codes from the May 20, 2025 patch are still available.

## Stadium Updates

### Stadium Rankings

We have increased the Stadium Ranked Skill Bonus from a cap of 25 to 50 for Pro, All-Star and Legend ranks. We are extending the “You’re an All-Star” challenge through the end of Season 17 and you’ll be able to earn the All-Star Juno Legendary skin either in Season 16 or Season 17.

*开发者注：Developer comments: We underestimated the amount of progress players would make in the higher Stadium leagues. We are boosting this bonus to speed up progression, allowing more players to advance to higher ranks quicker.*
