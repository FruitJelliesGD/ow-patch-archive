# Overwatch 2 Retail Patch Notes - April 25, 2025

> 日期: 2025-04-25 · 站点: en · 来源: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2025/04

## Hotfix Balance Update

This is a Hero balance hotfix. Replay codes from the April 22, 2025 patch are still available.

## Stadium Updates

Stadium Updates

## Tank（重装）

### D.Va（重装）

*开发者注：Developer Comments: We want to make sure that every Hero has answers to their potential counter so we're increasing D.Va's ability to combat Zarya’s beam damage.*

- Plot Armor – Rare Hero Survival Item
- Increased damage reduction against Beams to 65% (up from 30%).

## Damage（输出）

### Cassidy（输出）

*开发者注：Developer Comments: Every Hero generates Stadium Cash from dealing damage and healing at a rate unique to them which we’re monitoring carefully. We think we set the rate of Cassidy’s earnings too high and he's earning a bit too much Stadium Cash, allowing him to consistently get MVPs and buy late game Items early on.*

- Reduced the amount of Stadium Cash Cassidy gains from dealing damage and healing to 40% (down from 50%).

## General item Updates

General item Updates

## Weapon Items

### Salvaged Slugs – Epic Weapon Item

- Increased ammo restoration chance to 50% (up from 40%).
- Increased damage to barriers to 30% (up from 25%).

*开发者注：Developer Comments: Reinhardt and Zarya have been performing well so far. We're increasing the power of this Item to help players combat their powerful barriers.*

### Volskaya Ordnance – Epic Weapon Item

- Increased the weapon damage per 100 Max Life to 5% (up from 3%).
- Increased the extra weapon damage capacity to 20% (up from 15%).

*开发者注：Developer Comments: We've observed that sometimes tanks can be a bit too... well, tanky! Volskaya Ordnance was a tough Item to make work for not a big enough pay off, so we're adding a little bit of power here to make it a more efficient Item to buy in those cases.*

## Survival Items

### Iron Eyes – Rare Survival Item

- Damage Reduction against incoming Critical Hits increased to 20% (up from 15%).

*开发者注：Developer Comments: We're increasing the Damage Reduction of Iron Eyes to give players more powerful tools to combat builds with high burst damage.*

### Reinforced Titanium – Rare Survival Item

- Damage Reduction against incoming Ability Damage while you have Shields increased to 15% (up from 10%).

*开发者注：Developer Comments: We're increasing the Damage Reduction of Reinforced Titanium to give players more powerful tools to combat builds with high burst damage.*

## Bug Fixes

### General

- Fixed an issue where Competitive Points are not granted if the match ends early because one team left entirely.
- Fixed an issue where Overhealth is consumed on top of normal damage if the total amount of damage is more than the current amount of Overhealth on that hero.
