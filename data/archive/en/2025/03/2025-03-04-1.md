# Overwatch 2 Retail Patch Notes - March 4, 2025

> 日期: 2025-03-04 · 站点: en · 来源: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2025/03

## Bug Fix Update

This is a bug fix patch and contains no hero updates. In-Game Replay Codes and Highlights from the previous patch are removed.

## Bug Fixes

### General

- Fixed an issue which applied the galaxy weapon tint to other hero items.
- Fixed an issue which caused Ashe's default weapon skin to disappear from the weapon skin menu.
- Fixed an issue which would cause players to get stuck on the loot box screen if they were previously opening loot boxes before transitioning into a game.
