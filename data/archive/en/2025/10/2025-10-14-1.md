# Overwatch 2 Retail Patch Notes – October 14, 2025

> 日期: 2025-10-14 · 站点: en · 来源: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2025/10

## Season 19 – Haunted Masquerade

Unmask Your Power. Haunt the Competition. Season 19 creeps in... ambitious and cloaked in mystery! The Haunted Masquerade event introduces a spooktacular mode where mystical masks grant buffs, twisting every battle into a spectral spectacle. Plus, something wicked this way returns as Junkenstein's Revenge and Wrath of the Bride are back for frightful co-op fights. Then Stadium transforms under the eerie moonlight, unveiling new Heroes, a cursed map, fresh features, and match-defining Gadgets forged in shadow. Across the season, the festive spirit of Halloween stretches wide as Mythic cosmetics rise from the crypt, and a treasure trove of twisted tricks & treats awaits those brave enough to claim them. Welcome to the Haunted Masquerade, ENTER IF YOU DARE!

## New Arcade Game Mode – Haunted Masquerade

It's spooky time! The Haunted Masquerade is Season 19’s costumed centerpiece. Teams wear the mystical masks of currently allied Heroes, each granting buffs that can flip the fight for friends or foes and force fun new compositions. Everyone acquires special abilities based on whose mask they wear, with some granting specific benefits to lore linked Heroes and all of them rewarding synergy at every turn. Enter the Haunted Masquerade… IF YOU DARE!

## General Updates

### Hero Mastery Mode

- The Hero Mastery mode and courses are no longer available to play.

*开发者注：Developer comments: As we focus more time and effort into delivering the best experience for Quick Play, Competitive, Stadium, and other new ways to play, we’re going to be shifting focus away from past game modes that haven’t seen as much attention. With this in mind, we’ve made the decision to sunset Hero Mastery courses.*

### Team Text Chat

- New Options added to Social → Text Chat
- The color of each existing Text Chat Channel (Whisper/Group/Team/Match) can now be set. These default to their existing colors.
- The color Comms/Pings can now be set.
- Tabbing through each of the potential text chat channels includes a small new visual element to indicate that the channel has changed.

## Stadium Updates

### Stadium Competitive Updates

- Stadium Competitive will now use the best of 5 format.
- Powers are granted on rounds 1, 2, 3 and 5
- The first team that wins 3 rounds will win the match
- The mercy rule has been disabled
- Round 1 completion cash increased to 7500 (Up from 5000)
- Round 2 completion cash increased to 5000 (Up from 4000)
- Round complete cash increased to 4250 (Up from 3500)
- The amount of cash generated each round from healing and damage has been increased
- MVP bonus increased to 1500 (Up from 1000)

*开发者注：Developer comments: Now that stadium has been out for three seasons, players have a better understanding of the mode and how it works. Based on this, we think we can move away from the more segmented power curve offered by the 7-round approach, which served us well in these early months. We have been happy with the results of the Quick Play format in Season 18 and we want to bring those same wins to competitive. The goal of this change is to keep the competitive nature of the format while still making it move at a faster pace. We look forward to your feedback on this as we continue to iterate on Stadium and make it the best it can be.*

- Active in all Stadium matches and the Stadium Practice Range.
