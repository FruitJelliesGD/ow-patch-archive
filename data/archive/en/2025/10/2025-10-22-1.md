## Tank（重装）

### Sigma（重装）

*开发者注：Sigma’s recent buffs gave him a bit too much cosmic dominance, allowing him to stay in the air too long while dealing heavy burst damage. We’re dialing back Trinisphere and Zero Gravity slightly to keep his power in check while preserving the fun of his gravity-focused playstyle.*

- Trinisphere Decreased damage to 50% (Down from 75%).
- Decreased damage to 50% (Down from 75%).
- Zero Gravity Decreased damage reduction to 10% (Down from 15%).
- Decreased damage reduction to 10% (Down from 15%).

## Support（支援）

### Juno（支援）

*开发者注：Juno’s kit has been seeing high versatility, but a few powers weren’t keeping up with the pace of current matches. These updates give her stronger offensive and defensive options with more punch from range, faster follow-ups, and sturdier healing.*

- Rally Ring Increased cooldown reduction to 1.5s (Up from 1s).
- Increased cooldown reduction to 1.5s (Up from 1s).
- Hyper Healer Increased overhealth to 85 (Up from 75).
- Increased overhealth to 85 (Up from 75).
- Gravitational Push Increased attack speed to 35% (Up from 20%).
- Increased attack speed to 35% (Up from 20%).
- Long Range Blaster Increased weapon power to 20% (Up from 15%).
- Increased weapon power to 20% (Up from 15%).

### Zenyatta（支援）

*开发者注：Zenyatta’s Soul Control was letting players store too much ultimate energy, giving him access to Transcendence more often than intended. This adjustment lowers the cap slightly to keep his ultimate pacing in line with other supports while maintaining his calm, centered power curve.*

- Soul Control Decreased maximum ultimate charge to 65% (Down from 75%).
- Decreased maximum ultimate charge to 65% (Down from 75%).

## Bug Fixes

### General

- Capturing multikills is now correctly Play of Game's highest priority. This fix is applied to all game modes.

### Stadium Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue where lava spawned by Torbjorn's Power Magmini occasionally had full duration and damage.
- Fixed an issue where Torbjorn's Power Magmini would trigger On Ultimate Charge Spent effects.
- Fixed an issue where Refresher would permanently reduce the cooldown of both Genji and Pharah.
- Fixed an issue where picking Lucio's Power Reverb would change Soundwave's position in the HUD.
- Fixed an issue where Ana's biotic grenade with Falconer and Nanonade would give ultimate charge before the battle began.
- Fixed an issue where Hazard's Jagged Wall pre-placement would trigger on ability items.
