# Overwatch 2 Retail Patch Notes - November 11, 2025

> 日期: 2025-11-06 · 站点: en · 来源: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2025/11

## Story

A curated subset of lore videos is now watchable directly in the game. Be sure to check back when new lore beats are added!

## Spirit Showdown

From November 11-24, spirits decide your fate in the Arcade! Swap heroes when eliminating enemies or after dying two times.

As you complete challenges, you'll earn exclusive free Spirit-themed rewards!

## Junkrat's Loot Hunt

Junkrat's Loot Hunt is back again, running from November 20-23! Earn lootboxes by completing challenges that unlock as you play!

## General Updates

Player Progression

- Retired badges and sub-badges are accessible in the game again. Career Profile → Progression → Legacy.
- Overwatch 1 player level has also been moved to the Legacy screen.

Player Cards

- In Role Queue modes, instead of showing highest leveled heroes across the hero roster, player cards now show your highest leveled heroes in the role you are playing.
- Non-Role Queue modes will continue to show highest leveled heroes across the entire roster.

Advanced Info Panel

- Added the "Jump Cancel" keyword to abilities that can be ended early for bonus movement.

## Stadium Updates

### General Stadium Changes

- Drafts have been updated to use blind pick system. This means that both players on each time pick at the same time and their hero is revealed together.
- Draft mode now automatically assigns tanks to the 5th slot.
- Players are now able to unequip powers on the same round they are equipped.
- Powers are locked at the end of the Armory timer.
- HP bars are now displayed for your team in Stadium matches.
- The hero stat section in the Armory has been updated to sort by your highest stat increases.
- Detailed tooltips for each row in the Armory hero stat section have been added.
- Impacted abilities and weapons are displayed in these tooltips.
- Interact on any tooltip to access the full details screen.

### Stadium Combat Text Changes

- Weapon Power and Ability Power are now displayed as two separate floating numbers. This feature can be adjusted from Settings.
- A new setting has been added to adjust the lifetime of floating text.
- A new setting has been added to display self-healing. This setting is defaulted to off.
- We are now dynamically adjusting the height at which floating text is displayed to improve visibility for heroes in melee range.
- Overkill and overhealing values are now displayed when eliminating an enemy or healing a friendly target.
- Kill cameras will now display stadium combat text.
- Improved the stability of stadium combat text on moving targets so it’s less distracting.

## Bug Fixes

### General Fixes

- Fixed an issue with Wuyang and Illari that caused incorrect Healing and Resource rates to be applied when affected by Attack Speed increases.
- Fixed an issue where the Headhunter perk ignored headshot damage reduction from Bob's Tank passive.
- Fixed an issue where Reinhardt's Charge did not pull Wrecking Ball out of Roll mode.
- Fixed an issue where Mei's Deep Freeze perk could potentially freeze enemies twice within a short timeframe.
- Fixed an issue where characters affected by Ana's Groggy were still slowed after using an ability that should have cleansed the Groggy effect.
- Fixed an issue where Sojourn's charge was lost after a primary fire when using the Overcharged perk.
- Fixed an issue where the timer for Mercy's resurrect was not shown in ping messages.
- Fixed several minor color and formatting issues in Advanced Info Panel text.

### Stadium Fixes

- Fixed an issue where Kiriko's clone could inadvertently stop the payload from moving due to too many friendly players on the objective.
- Fixed an issue where the Salvaged Slug item interrupted Winston's primary fire to reload the granted ammo.
- Fixed an issue where Reaper's aim was not accurate immediately after using Shadow Step.
- Fixed an issue where the size of the mini Steel Trap from Junkrat's "Trap II, Esquire" power was not accurate relative to its trigger timing.
- Fixed an issue where Hazard's Downpour ability did not gain benefits from life steal bonuses.
- Fixed an issue where Torbjorn's Clocked Power gave the benefit to all allied heroes instead of just Torbjorn.
