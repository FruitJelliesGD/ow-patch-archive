# Overwatch 2 Retail Patch Notes - November 25, 2025

> 日期: 2025-11-25 · 站点: en · 来源: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2025/11

## General Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue where Junkrat's mythic skin could cause the game to freeze when selecting certain components.

## Stadium Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue in Stadium where Lucio's Radio Edit power remained active even after being removed from the build loadout. This power is now available again.
- Fixed an issue where Winston's Salvaged Slugs mod did not reduce his ammo when using primary fire.
