# Overwatch Retail Patch Notes – June 9, 2021

> 日期: 2021-06-09 · 站点: en · 来源: https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2021/06

## Bug Fixes

### General

- Fixed a bug that awarded the wrong Player Icon to players for Season 4 of Competitive 6v6 Lockout Elimination
- Fixed a bug that caused player ranks to not appear properly on the hero select and tab screens

### Heroes

Sombra

- Fixed a bug that caused Sombra’s neck to be outstretched during her "Pumpkinette" victory pose

Torbjörn

- Fixed a bug that caused Torbjörn’s fingers to clip through his mallet during his "Taking a Break" victory pose
